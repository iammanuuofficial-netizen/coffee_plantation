welcome to green hills coffee plantation

green hills coffee plantation is an estimed coffee plantation that was established in the 1900s by the british settlers.it is located in kericho county.The settlers found it suitable to establish the farm there since it was located in the highland area which was suitable for coffee farming

coffee varieties

  • Arabica
  • Robusta
  • Batian
  • Ruiru

coffee production process

    1. Planting - planting coffee seedling in the nursery and farm
    2. Harvesting - picking ripe red coffee cherries by hand
    3. drying - sun drying the cherries to reduce moisture
    4. packaging - packaging ready for the market

    coffee plantation activities

    PRUNING:
    pruning is the selective removal of specific parts of a plant—such as branches, buds, stems, or roots—to improve its health, shape, and productivity.
    HARVESTING:
    Harvesting is the process of gathering mature crops in this case mature coffee cherries from the coffee tree.
    DRYING:
    The drying of coffee is a critical post-harvest step where newly harvested coffee beans or cherries are reduced from a high moisture content.
